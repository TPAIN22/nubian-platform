# Nubian — Go-to-Market Plan

**Founder location:** Jeddah, Saudi Arabia
**Target market (Phase 1):** Sudanese sellers & buyers in the Gulf (Jeddah first)
**Target market (Phase 2):** Sudan diaspora → Sudan corridor (gifts/essentials home)
**Target market (Phase 3):** Inside Sudan (Port Sudan, Wad Madani, Kassala)

---

## Positioning

> **"The marketplace for Sudanese sellers and buyers — in the Gulf and back home."**

Not "Sudan e-commerce." The Gulf wedge is your beachhead because payments work, shipping works, and customers have disposable income. Sudan comes later, once you have liquidity.

---

## Phase 0 — Pre-launch checklist (Week 0)

Block on these before recruiting any merchant. Without them, you'll burn trust.

- [ ] **Payments**: mada + Apple Pay + STC Pay live in checkout. Cards alone is not enough for KSA.
- [ ] **Bankak** integration planned for Phase 2 (diaspora → Sudan). Skip for now.
- [ ] **Shipping**: pick one — SMSA, Aramex, or SPL. Get a business account. Negotiate rates.
- [ ] **Arabic UI** verified end-to-end on mobile + dashboard (RTL, fonts, checkout flow).
- [ ] **Merchant onboarding flow** tested by you, end-to-end, on a real phone in <10 minutes.
- [ ] **Commercial registration (CR)** in KSA, or a partner who has one — required to accept mada at scale.
- [ ] **WhatsApp Business** number for support (Sudanese culture = WhatsApp > email).

## Phase 1 — First 10 merchants (Weeks 1–4)

**Goal:** 10 active merchants in Jeddah, each with ≥10 live products, ≥1 real order each.

This phase is **manual and in-person**. Do not try to scale with ads or automation. Trust is the bottleneck, not traffic.

### Week 1 — Map the community

- [ ] Visit 3 Sudanese restaurants/cafés in **Al-Ruwais, Al-Safa, Al-Aziziyah** on Friday/Saturday. Talk to 10+ people. Ask: *"من بتعرفه بيبيع أونلاين؟"*
- [ ] Join these Facebook groups:
  - السودانيين في جدة
  - سودانيز في السعودية
  - سوق السودانيين بجدة
  - سوق السودان (main one)
- [ ] Join 5+ Sudanese WhatsApp commerce groups (ask friends/family to add you).
- [ ] Build a spreadsheet: **Merchant Lead List**
  - Name, IG handle, what they sell, follower count, phone, status (cold → contacted → onboarded)
  - Target: 50 leads by end of Week 1.

### Week 2 — Recruit

- [ ] Make a **60-second selfie video** in Sudanese Arabic showing how easy listing a product is. Phone quality, not corporate.
- [ ] Post the video in all groups joined in Week 1.
- [ ] DM 50 sellers from your lead list with the video + this pitch:

  > *"السلام عليكم، أنا [اسمك] من منصة Nubian. شفت بتبيع [منتجك] على الإنستا — حنا بنبني سوق إلكتروني للسودانيين في الخليج والسودان. بنوفر لك متجر، دفع مدى وآبل باي، وشحن. مفي رسوم تسجيل. خصم بس لما تبيع. ممكن نتقابل 15 دقيقة قهوة؟"*

- [ ] Target: **10 in-person meetings booked.**

### Week 3 — Onboard in person

- [ ] Meet each merchant **at their home or favorite café**. Buy the coffee.
- [ ] Onboard them yourself — sit beside them, upload their catalog from their phone gallery, write the product descriptions, set prices.
- [ ] Do not leave until they have **at least 10 products live**.
- [ ] Get them to post their store link in their own IG story / WhatsApp status that same day.
- [ ] Target: **5 merchants fully onboarded.**

### Week 4 — First orders & second cohort

- [ ] Buy from each merchant yourself (small orders). Test the full flow: order → payment → notification → fulfillment → delivery.
- [ ] Document every friction point. Fix the worst 3 by end of week.
- [ ] Onboard 5 more merchants using lessons learned. Total: **10 active merchants.**
- [ ] Get a written testimonial (or short video) from your 2 favorite merchants.

## Phase 2 — Liquidity & diaspora corridor (Weeks 5–12)

**Goal:** 50 merchants, 500 orders/month, diaspora-to-Sudan shipping live.

### Demand side

- [ ] Recruit **5 Sudanese micro-influencers** in the Gulf (1k–50k followers — perfume reviewers, abaya stylists, food creators). Pay **20% commission** through your existing `/affiliate/*` flow.
- [ ] Run **one paid campaign** on Meta targeting Sudanese in KSA/UAE/Qatar (interests: Sudan, Sudanese music/food, Arabic + English speakers in Gulf). Budget: **$300 max**. Measure CAC.
- [ ] Launch a **referral program**: customers get SAR 20 credit when a friend completes their first order. Use `referralTracking` you already built.

### Supply side

- [ ] Scale merchant recruitment from 1:1 to 1:many: **weekly merchant onboarding webinar** on Zoom, Saturday afternoons.
- [ ] Add merchants in **Riyadh and Dammam** (visit once each, or recruit a part-time community manager in each city).
- [ ] Build a public **merchant leaderboard** — Sudanese culture rewards visible status. Top sellers of the month get featured.

### Sudan corridor

- [ ] Partner with **one shipping consolidator** running Jeddah → Port Sudan (there are several Sudanese-owned ones — ask around in Al-Ruwais).
- [ ] Launch "**Send to family in Sudan**" as a checkout option: diaspora pays in SAR, recipient gets goods in Sudan within 7–14 days.
- [ ] Add **Bankak** as a payout method for any Sudan-based merchants you onboard.

## Phase 3 — Inside Sudan (Months 4–6)

Only start this when Phase 2 is generating ≥SAR 50k/month GMV.

- [ ] Hire **1 community manager in Port Sudan** (Sudanese, ideally someone with retail/logistics background).
- [ ] Onboard 20 merchants in Port Sudan using the same in-person playbook.
- [ ] Bankak is mandatory by now. SDG pricing live.
- [ ] Expand to Wad Madani, then Kassala.

---

## Categories to prioritize (in order)

Based on what actually moves in Sudanese diaspora commerce:

1. **Perfumes & bukhoor** — highest margin, easiest to ship, biggest demand
2. **Abayas & thobes** — Sudanese styles unavailable in Gulf malls
3. **Dilka, henna, traditional cosmetics** — strong word-of-mouth category
4. **Traditional food & spices** (well-bottled, shelf-stable only) — strong diaspora demand
5. **Baby & kids essentials** — recurring revenue, diaspora-to-Sudan strong

Don't try to be a general marketplace early. Pick 2–3 categories, dominate them, expand later.

## What NOT to do

- **No paid ads in Phase 1.** Traffic to an empty marketplace = burned money + bad reputation.
- **No expanding beyond Jeddah in Phase 1.** Depth > breadth. One city dominated beats five cities dabbled in.
- **No general-marketplace mindset.** Focus on Sudanese-specific products that no one else in the Gulf sells well.
- **No building new features in Phase 1.** Your platform is already over-built for 10 merchants. Sell, don't code.
- **No remote-only merchant onboarding in Phase 1.** Show up in person. This is non-negotiable in Sudanese culture.
- **No Bankak in Phase 1.** Add it when you actually have Sudan-based merchants or diaspora corridor demand.

## Weekly cadence

Every Sunday evening, 30 minutes:

1. Update the **Merchant Lead List** sheet.
2. Update the **Metrics** sheet.
3. Pick the **one bottleneck** for the coming week. Only one.
4. Plan in-person merchant meetings for the week.
5. Ship one product improvement that removes friction reported by a real merchant.
